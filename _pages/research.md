My research is centered on how turbulent space plasmas transfer energy across scales and between fields, flows, and particles. I combine **kinetic simulations**, **spacecraft observations**, and **theoretical/statistical diagnostics**.

## Solar-wind turbulence

**Scientific question:** How do observational averaging, turbulence, and local Alfvénic conditions influence our interpretation of near-Sun plasma dynamics?

Using Parker Solar Probe observations, I investigate correlation scales, switchbacks, radial trends, and sub-Alfvénic intervals. A recurring theme is the distinction between physically meaningful scale dependence and analysis choices such as averaging windows and data cadence.

*Methods:* PSP magnetic-field and plasma observations, autocorrelation functions, interval selection, Alfvén Mach number analysis, and comparisons with turbulence scaling arguments.

## Magnetic reconnection and energy conversion

**Scientific question:** Where and at what scales is bulk-flow energy converted into particle internal energy in turbulent collisionless plasmas?

Using P3D and VPIC particle-in-cell simulations, I examine the contributions of pressure dilatation and deviatoric pressure–strain interactions, including their spatial intermittency, association with reconnecting structures, and dependence on filtering scale.

$$-\mathbf{P}:\nabla\mathbf{u}=-p\,\theta-\boldsymbol{\Pi}:\mathbf{D}.$$

*Methods:* Kinetic simulations, pressure-tensor diagnostics, velocity gradients, structure functions, scale filtering, and spatially conditioned statistics.

## Kinetic boundary layers and MMS

**Scientific question:** How do shear-driven turbulent boundary layers redistribute energy, and how do their energy-conversion signatures compare between in situ observations and kinetic simulations?

I study Kelvin–Helmholtz intervals observed by the Magnetospheric Multiscale (MMS) mission, including boundary-aligned coordinates, velocity-dependent distributions, and ion/electron pressure–strain contributions.

*Methods:* MMS plasma and field observations, LMN transformations, event-by-event normalization, two-dimensional statistical maps, and kinetic simulations.

## Computational and numerical methods

I develop reusable methods for plasma simulation analysis, including multidimensional xarray data structures, periodic spatial derivatives, tensor diagnostics, Fourier/Gaussian filters, and reproducible visualizations.
