## Research appointments

### Research Assistant — University of Delaware Space Physics Group (May 2021–present)
**Advisor:** Prof. William H. Matthaeus

- Investigate energy conversion and dissipation in collisionless plasma turbulence, including nonlinear evolution and kinetic-scale processes.
- Analyze pressure–strain interactions as a diagnostic of flow-to-internal-energy conversion.
- Study the Alfvénic transition, velocity shear, magnetic deflections, and switchbacks using Parker Solar Probe observations.
- Conduct statistical studies connecting solar-wind turbulence and magnetic reconnection.

### Graduate Research Fellow — Los Alamos National Laboratory, T2–Plasma Astrophysics Group (May 2022–September 2024)
**CSES Fellowship · Advisor:** Dr. Fan Guo

- Conducted large-scale VPIC particle-in-cell simulations of shear-driven turbulence and magnetic reconnection.
- Studied energy dissipation, intermittency, reconnection-driven heating, and the statistics of kinetic dissipation structures.

### Science Undergraduate Laboratory Intern — Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility, Experimental Hall B (Summers 2019 and 2020)
**Advisor:** Dr. Nathan Baltzell

- Developed real-time hardware-performance access for the CLAS12 Ring Imaging Cherenkov detector (RICH).
